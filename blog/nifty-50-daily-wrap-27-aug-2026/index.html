Market Summary — 26 Aug 2026

The mood turned cautious as Nifty 50 slipped 127 points to close at 24,207.75, with selling pressure evident across 36 of 50 stocks. Weak global cues and profit-booking in heavyweights dragged the index lower.

Kotak Bank stole the spotlight, surging 3.76% to ₹416.7 on strong volumes of over 2 crore shares — the highest turnover in the index at ₹832.76 Cr. This came on the back of easing concerns around its leadership transition and improving asset quality A clear sector rotation played out — money moved out of defensive IT and FMCG (Nestle down 1.86%) into rate-sensitive financials and cyclical metals. Metal stocks led gains with a 1.27% rise, while private banks and PSU banks both closed in the green.

Watch if this rotation into financials and domestic cyclicals sustains, or if global uncertainty triggers deeper cuts ahead.
