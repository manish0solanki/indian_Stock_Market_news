Market Summary — 27 Aug 2026

Markets closed cautious as Nifty 50 shed 117 points to end at 24,090.85, down 0.48%. Bears dominated breadth with 31 stocks falling against just 18 gainers, signalling weak conviction even as the index held above 24,000.

Adani twins stole the show — Adani Enterprises surged 1.83% to ₹3,169 and Adani Ports added 1.33% to ₹1,714, lifting sentiment in the broader group. Kotak Mahindra Bank gained 1.8% to ₹424.2 on strong volumes of over 3.28 crore shares. On the flip side, HDFC Bank cracked 2.23% to ₹711 with heavy turnover of ₹3,328 Cr — its weakest showing among heavyweights and a drag on financials. Hindalco tumbled 2.75% to ₹1,024 as metal demand worries resurfaced.

Sector rotation was clear: defensives ruled with Pharma (+0.84%) and Healthcare (+0.73%) leading, while PSU Banks (-0.94%) and IT (-0.32%) faced selling. Private Banks barely stayed green at +0.09%, split by Kotak's rally versus HDFC Bank's fall.
