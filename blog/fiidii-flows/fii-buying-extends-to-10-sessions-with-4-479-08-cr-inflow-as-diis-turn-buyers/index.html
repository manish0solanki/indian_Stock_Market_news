🏦 Flow Analysis

Institutional investors delivered a powerful show of support on 25 Nov 2026, with foreign and domestic desks together pumping ₹8,497.8 Cr into the cash market. Foreign institutional investors bought shares worth ₹24,309.87 Cr and sold ₹19,830.79 Cr, leaving a net inflow of ₹4,479.08 Cr. Domestic institutional investors were also net buyers, purchasing ₹22,379.82 Cr against sales of ₹18,361.1 Cr for a net ₹4,018.72 Cr. The Nifty 50 rallied 1.85% to close at 25,819.25, matching the strong flow backdrop.

The foreign bid extended a buying streak to 10 sessions, while the domestic figure marked just one day of buying. That contrast matters because the previous session on 23-Nov-2026 showed the opposite split: FIIs were net buyers of ₹5,672.99 Cr, but DIIs were net sellers of ₹2,760.12 Cr. Domestic participation therefore added a fresh layer of support rather than simply repeating the recent pattern.

The size of the foreign inflow was smaller than in the previous session, yet the combined total remained substantial because both groups bought together. Over the last five sessions, FIIs have contributed net inflows of ₹33,738.68 Cr, while DIIs have recorded net outflows of ₹8,117.67 Cr. For an ordinary investor, that gap shows foreign money has been the steadier force recently, even though domestic flows can shift quickly from one session to the next.

Next, watch whether the FII buying streak continues, whether DII buying extends beyond a single session, and whether the Nifty holds above its latest close of 25,819.25. The key signal will be if index strength keeps aligning with broad institutional support.
