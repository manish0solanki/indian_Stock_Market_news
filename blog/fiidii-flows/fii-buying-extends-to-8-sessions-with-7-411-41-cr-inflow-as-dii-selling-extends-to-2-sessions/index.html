🏦 Flow Analysis

Foreign investors extended their buying streak to eight sessions on 19 Nov 2026, pouring a net ₹7,411.41 Cr into the cash market and overpowering continued selling by domestic institutions.

The scale of overseas support stood out. FIIs bought shares worth ₹20,706.95 Cr and sold ₹13,295.54 Cr, leaving net purchases above the previous session’s ₹5,866.78 Cr on 18-Nov-2026. Their net buying over the last five sessions reached ₹28,074.8 Cr. Domestic institutions moved the other way: purchases of ₹17,651.5 Cr fell short of sales of ₹19,008.07 Cr, producing net selling of ₹1,356.57 Cr and extending their selling streak to two sessions. That was smaller than the previous session’s net selling of ₹3,159.9 Cr, while their five-session net total stood at ₹10,092.17 Cr on the selling side.

Together, the two groups added a net ₹6,054.84 Cr to the cash market. The Nifty 50 closed at 25,514.3, up 1.14%, so the benchmark advanced alongside strong combined institutional inflows. For ordinary investors, the key distinction is between gross activity and net flow: both sides traded heavily, but FIIs bought substantially more than they sold, while DIIs did the opposite. The figures are NSE provisional cash-market data and describe institutional activity, not the position of every market participant.
