🏦 Flow Analysis

Foreign investors stepped up their buying on 12 Nov 2026, but domestic institutions continued to sell, leaving the Nifty 50 with only a modest gain.

FIIs bought shares worth ₹20,273.32 Cr and sold ₹12,442.72 Cr, resulting in net purchases of ₹7,830.6 Cr. This extended their buying streak to three sessions. However, the net inflow was smaller than the ₹12,563.33 Cr recorded on 11-Nov-2026. DIIs moved in the opposite direction, purchasing ₹15,855.13 Cr and selling ₹22,186.3 Cr, for a net outflow of ₹6,331.17 Cr. Their selling streak has now reached 10 sessions, though the latest net sale was smaller than the previous session’s ₹8,434.53 Cr.

Together, the two groups provided a combined net inflow of ₹1,499.43 Cr. The Nifty 50 closed at 25,299.35, up 0.18%, indicating a positive but restrained session for the benchmark. For retail investors, the figures show that foreign buying outweighed domestic selling, but not by a wide margin. A streak describes the direction of net flows over consecutive sessions; it does not, by itself, show which shares or sectors attracted the money.

The recent pattern remains divided. Across the last five sessions, FIIs recorded net purchases totalling ₹41,355.72 Cr, while DIIs posted net sales of ₹28,607.24 Cr. The next data points to watch are whether the FII buying streak continues, whether DII selling eases after 10 sessions, and whether the combined net flow remains positive alongside further movement in the Nifty. These are NSE provisional cash-market figures.
