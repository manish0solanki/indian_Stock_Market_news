Market Summary — 16 Oct 2026

Bullish close for Nifty as the index reclaimed 23,100 with a 149-point gain. IT stocks led the charge after strong US cues and fresh deal wins reignited buying interest.

Tech Mahindra stole the show, surging 8.5% to ₹1,790 with massive turnover of ₹3,657 Cr — its highest single-day gain in months. TCS and Infosys weren't far behind, adding 4.9% and 4.6% respectively. The IT pack alone contributed most of Nifty's upside, jumping nearly 4%. This matters because IT had been lagging for weeks; today's move suggests FPIs may be returning to quality large-caps.

On the flip side, FMCG was the lone loser, down 0.4%. Tata Consumer slipped 1.3% as rural demand worries persist. Indigo fell 1.9% on profit-booking after recent highs. Breadth was nearly balanced at 26:24, so this wasn't a broad-based rally — it was concentrated in tech.

Sector rotation is visible: money is moving from defensives (FMCG) to growth plays (IT, Media). Auto and Metal also saw selective buying.
