Market Summary — 24 Sept 2026

The mood turned sharply bearish as the NIFTY 50 crashed 383.7 points to close at 23,063.1, a steep 1.64% drop. Breadth was dreadful — just 3 stocks advanced against 47 decliners, with zero unchanged. This was a broad-based selloff with nowhere to hide.

Financials led the carnage, plunging 2.39% and dragging the index down. HDFC Life nosedived 6.16% to ₹526.9 on heavy volume of nearly 1.8 crore shares, while Bajaj Finance cracked 5.87% to ₹982 — both reflecting panic in the insurance and NBFC space. Axis Bank and Bajaj Finserv also shed over 4.5% each. Even the "best" sector, IT, fell 0.44%, showing defensive buying merely cushioned losses rather than delivered gains. Cipla, ONGC and NTPC were the rare green spots, but their rises were timid.

The rotation out of high-beta financials into defensives like pharma and IT suggests investors are bracing for turbulence ahead. With FII flows likely under pressure and global cues soft, caution is warranted.
