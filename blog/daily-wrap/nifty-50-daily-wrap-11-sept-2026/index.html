Market Summary — 11 Sept 2026

Markets closed cautious as the NIFTY 50 shed 79.7 points to end at 23,398.1. The advance-decline ratio was weak at 12 rising against 37 falling stocks, showing broad-based selling pressure.

HDFCBANK was the standout gainer, rallying 2.08% to ₹708.25 on heavy volumes of over 3.14 crore shares. This single stock lent crucial support to the index and propped up the Private Bank space, which emerged as the lone bright spot in an otherwise dull session. On the flip side, metals took a beating — Hindalco crashed 3.21% and JSW Steel fell nearly 3%, dragging the broader market mood down. Realty was the worst hit, sinking 2.7%.

Sector rotation is clearly visible: money is moving out of cyclicals like metals and real estate into defensive banking and select IT names like TechM and Wipro, which closed marginally higher. Pharma turned mixed despite Dr Reddy's nearly 2% rise.

With global cues uncertain and domestic breadth this weak, stay stock-specific and avoid aggressive bets until the NIFTY reclaims 23,500 convincingly.
