Market Summary — 11 Sept 2026

Markets stayed cautious with NIFTY closing marginally up at 23,477.8 (+46 points). Breadth was nearly split — 24 stocks rose, 26 fell — signalling no clear conviction.

HDFC Life led gains, jumping 2.36% to ₹526 on strong volumes, while PowerGrid rallied 2.22% to ₹271.75 as PSU infrastructure names found buyers. Bharti Airtel too added 1.34%, crossing ₹1,839, helped by steady telecom traction.

On the flip side, IT came under pressure. HCLTech dropped 1.85% to ₹1,207, the worst NIFTY loser, tracking weak global tech cues. Metals cracked as well — Hindalco and TataSteel shed 1.26% and 1.04% respectively on softening commodity prices and China demand worries.

Sector rotation was clear: money moved from metals and IT into financials and PSUs. Financial Services topped with +0.57%, while Media and PSU Banks joined the green. Metal was the worst hit at -0.65%.

For retail investors, the message is stock-specific action over broad momentum.
