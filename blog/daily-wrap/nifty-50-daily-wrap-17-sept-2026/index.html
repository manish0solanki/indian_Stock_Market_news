Market Summary — 17 Sept 2026

Markets closed on a cautiously optimistic note with the NIFTY 50 inching up 53 points to 23,270.6. Breadth favoured the bulls at 36 advances versus 14 declines, though the narrow 0.23% gain suggests buyers lacked conviction at higher levels.

The standout story was insurance stocks catching fire. HDFC Life surged 5.05% to ₹557 on heavy volumes of nearly 94 lakh shares, while SBI Life jumped 4.06% to ₹1,766.9. Both stocks contributed significantly to the NIFTY's gains and signal renewed institutional interest in the financial savings space. Dr Reddy's also shone, up 3.07%, riding the pharma wave.

Sector rotation was clearly visible. Pharma (+1.66%) and Healthcare (+1.56%) topped the charts, while Private Banks (-0.42%) dragged. HDFC Bank alone shed 1.18% despite ₹1,542 Cr turnover, reflecting profit-booking in heavyweights. ONGC (-1.85%) and Titan (-1.38%) were other notable laggards.

Defensive sectors leading and banking cooling off hints at a risk-off tilt among smart money.
