📝 Market Wrap

Banks powered a sharp headline rally on Dalal Street, even as most stocks across the wider market ended lower. The Nifty 50 jumped 286.3 points, or 1.12%, to close at 25800.6, while the Sensex rose 1.08% to 83914.81. The Nifty Bank surged 2.49% to 64635.2 and Nifty Financial Services gained 2.02% to 28871.2, comfortably outpacing the Nifty Midcap 50’s 0.73% rise to 17885.85.

Private Bank led the sectoral table with a 3.01% advance, followed by Bank at 2.49% and Financial Services at 2.02%. Realty added 1.71%, while IT and Services Sector each climbed 1.3%. Axis Bank rallied 4.72% to ₹1446, but Apollo Hospitals Enterprise topped the gainers, jumping 5.29% to ₹9200. Max Healthcare Institute rose 3.88% to ₹1081, Maruti Suzuki India gained 3.11% to ₹14011, and UltraTech Cement advanced 3.02% to ₹12939.

The rally was much narrower than the Nifty’s jump suggests. Within the index, 32 stocks advanced and 18 declined, but across the NSE, 881 shares rose against 1721 declines, with 55 unchanged. For an ordinary investor, that breadth reading means falling stocks heavily outnumbered gainers beyond the large index heavyweights. Media slumped 1.68%, Consumer Durables slipped 0.4%, Metal eased 0.23%, Energy lost 0.22%, and FMCG edged down 0.06%. Titan Company fell 1.7% to ₹4620, Tata Steel dropped 1.6% to ₹178, NTPC declined 1.08% to ₹321.5, Hindustan Unilever lost 1.05% to ₹2052.5, and Jio Financial Services slipped 0.81% to ₹224.97.
