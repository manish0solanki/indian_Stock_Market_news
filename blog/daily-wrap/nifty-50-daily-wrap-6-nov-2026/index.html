📝 Market Wrap

Private banks powered a sharp Nifty rally on 6 Nov 2026, even as most shares across the exchange declined. The Nifty 50 jumped 493.75 points, or 2.01%, to close at 25,030.35. The Sensex rose 2.43% to 81,237.3, while Nifty Bank gained 2.27% and Nifty Financial Services advanced 2.57%.

The headline move was much stronger than the market beneath it. Within the Nifty 50, 44 stocks advanced and only six declined, showing broad support among index heavyweights. Across all NSE stocks, however, 1,714 declined against 880 advances, with 57 unchanged. For an ordinary investor, that gap means a portfolio could have lagged the index despite the strong close. Nifty Midcap 50’s modest 0.33% rise reinforced the narrow nature of the rally.

Private Bank led sectors with a 3.22% gain, ahead of Financial Services and Bank. Consumer Durables rose 1.7%, Services Sector added 1.63%, and India Consumption gained 1.29%. Media fell 1.52%, Energy slipped 0.05%, and Realty managed just 0.09%. Among stocks, Mahindra & Mahindra surged 5.11% to ₹3,287.2, ICICI Bank rallied 4.75% to ₹1,571.2, and HDFC Life gained 3.95% to ₹565.3. HDFC Bank rose 3.72% and Titan added 3.42%. Losses were limited among the biggest laggards: Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles fell 0.58%, Eicher Motors 0.56%, Power Grid 0.48%, Shriram Finance 0.3% and Wipro 0.2%.

Next, watch whether exchange-wide breadth improves, midcaps catch up, and banks continue leading; those readings will show whether the index’s strength is spreading beyond its largest constituents.
