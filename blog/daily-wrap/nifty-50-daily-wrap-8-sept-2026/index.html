Market Summary — 08 Sept 2026

The mood turned cautious as Nifty 50 shed 144 points to close at 23,635 — a 0.61% drop with nearly twice as many stocks declining as advancing. Private banks led the drag, falling almost 1%, while media stocks offered the only bright spot.

What moved: Defence heavyweight BEL rallied 1.6% to ₹410.55 on strong volumes of over 2.3 crore shares, signalling continued retail and institutional appetite for PSU defence plays. On the flip side, ICICI Bank tumbled nearly 2% to ₹1,399.4 with heavy turnover of ₹1,700 Cr — the banking giant's fall pulled the entire financial pack lower, with Axis Bank also slipping 1.7%.

Sector rotation watch: Money quietly shifted to defensives — FMCG, pharma and healthcare all closed in the green. Hindustan Unilever gained 1% to ₹1,980, while ONGC rose 1% on firm crude cues. This flight to safety suggests investors are booking profits in high-beta names and parking funds in steadier counters.
