📝 Market Wrap

A technology-led rally lifted the Nifty 50 by 0.51% to 23,053.1, even as the broader market sank under a wave of declines. The index added 116.35 points, but the Sensex’s 0.42% gain masked a much weaker session beyond the large caps: across the NSE, 1,996 stocks fell against just 583 advances, with 50 unchanged. Within the Nifty itself, breadth was only mildly positive at 29 advances and 21 declines, showing how heavily the headline gain depended on a handful of winners.

IT did the heavy lifting, jumping 2.32%, with Wipro up 4.32% at ₹198.31 and Infosys gaining 3.36% to ₹1,185. Healthcare rose 1.58% and Pharma 1.55%, helped by Apollo Hospitals Enterprise’s 3.02% rise to ₹8,448. Trent led the gainers, surging 5.44% to ₹3,017.3, while InterGlobe Aviation climbed 4.33% to ₹4,877.6. For retail investors, that combination signals narrow leadership rather than a confident, market-wide rally.

Financials offered little support. Nifty Bank slipped 0.41% to 54,686.95, Nifty Fin Service managed only 0.03%, and Private Bank ended flat. The damage was wider elsewhere: Consumer Durables lost 1.78%, Media 1.46%, Energy 0.97%, PSU Bank 0.93% and Auto 0.87%, while Nifty Midcap 50 fell 0.86% to 16,513.3. Shriram Finance was the biggest listed loser, down 2.13% at ₹823.15, followed by Nestle India, off 1.82% at ₹1,167.

Next, watch whether advances improve from 583 and whether banks and midcaps stop lagging. If IT and healthcare remain the only strong pillars while declines stay near 1,996, the market’s mood remains defensive beneath the Nifty’s green close.
