📝 Market Wrap

A broad sell-off dragged the Nifty 50 lower on 18 Nov 2026, with losses stretching well beyond the headline index. The benchmark closed at 25,227.95, down 159.45 points or 0.63%, while the Sensex slipped 0.56% to 82,068.77. The Nifty Midcap 50 fell harder, losing 1.33%, a sign that the pressure was not confined to large caps.

The breadth made that clear. Only 15 Nifty stocks advanced against 35 declines, and across the NSE 726 shares rose while 1,877 fell, with 52 unchanged. For an ordinary investor, that means the typical listed stock had a much weaker day than the index’s 0.63% fall alone suggests. Financials offered limited support: Nifty Bank declined 0.38% to 62,038.65, but Nifty Financial Services dropped 0.72%.

State Bank of India led the gainers, rising 2.38% to ₹1,135, helping PSU Bank emerge as the strongest sector with a 0.59% gain. Cipla added 2.09%, while Media, Pharma and Healthcare also closed higher. At the other end, Consumer Durables slumped 3.13% and Metal lost 2.46%. Titan was the biggest Nifty loser, down 5.3% at ₹4,648.10, followed by HDFC Life, Hindalco, Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles and Wipro. Auto, IT and India Consumption also weakened, leaving defensive pockets rather than cyclical sectors in control.

Next, watch whether NSE breadth improves, whether midcaps stop underperforming, and whether PSU banks and healthcare can retain their relative strength. Also track the 25,227.95 closing level and whether financial services joins the Bank index in steadying.
