Market Summary — 19 Oct 2026

The mood turned cautious as NIFTY 50 shed 257 points to close at 22,888.95, down 1.11%. Breadth was weak with just 9 stocks advancing against 41 decliners — a clear sign of selling pressure across the board.

Key movers: Axis Bank crashed 4.19% to ₹1,236, dragging the private banking pack down. Weak Q2 loan growth fears and margin compression worries hit the stock hard. On the flip side, SBI rose 1.15% to ₹973.7 with strong ₹1,467 Cr turnover, riding the PSU Bank rally. This split tells a story — investors are rotating from expensive private lenders to cheaper PSU names.

Sector rotation: PSU Banks shone with a 2.46% gain, while Private Banks sank 1.85%. Metals and Energy also slipped despite JSW Steel gaining 2.06%. ONGC's 1.35% rise couldn't lift the Energy index. IT bellwether Infosys dropped 2.63% to ₹1,130.7, adding to the gloom.
