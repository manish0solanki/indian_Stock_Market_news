Market Summary — 01 Sept 2026

Markets stayed cautious today with NIFTY 50 slipping 25 points to close at 24,055.8. Breadth was weak — only 20 stocks rose against 30 decliners — suggesting underlying selling pressure despite the flat headline.

Key movers: ITC stole the spotlight, surging 4.3% to ₹266.6 on heavy volumes of 3.1 crore shares. The FMCG major's breakout signals renewed institutional interest in defensives. Bharti Airtel too rallied 3.6% to ₹1,877, riding telecom tariff hike optimism. On the flip side, Maruti crashed 4.4% to ₹12,950 — a ₹597 drop — as weak August auto sales spooked investors. Shriram Finance plunged 4.6%, reflecting NBFC stress.

Sector rotation: IT (+0.98%) and FMCG (+0.94%) led gains as money fled riskier pockets. Healthcare (-1.6%) was the worst hit, with Max Health down 3.75%. Notably, Reliance bucked the trend with a 2.5% rise, offering index support.
