📝 Market Wrap

The Nifty 50 surged 1.85% to 25,819.25 on 25 Nov 2026, but the headline rally masked a divided market where defensive consumption and financial stocks did the heavy lifting while the broader universe struggled. The index added 469.6 points, outpacing the Sensex’s 1.97% gain to 84,061.07, yet the advance was far from universal.

Within the 50-share benchmark, 42 stocks advanced against just 8 declines, painting a picture of robust strength. However, the wider NSE market told a different story: 1,570 stocks fell compared with 1,027 gainers, with 62 unchanged. This divergence means the average investor’s portfolio likely underperformed the headline index unless heavily weighted toward the day’s leaders. The Nifty Midcap 50 managed only a 0.46% rise, confirming that the rally was concentrated in large caps rather than spreading across the market.

FMCG led the charge with a 3.79% jump, powered by Hindustan Unilever’s 6.08% surge to ₹2,141.8 and Tata Consumer Products’ 4.66% gain. Pharma added 2.38%, while Private Bank and Financial Services climbed 2.21% and 2.17% respectively, helping Nifty Fin Service outperform with a 2.17% rise. At the other end, Media slumped 4.44% and Metal dropped 2.04%, with Tata Steel falling 2.27% and Hindalco declining 1.17%. HDFC Life Insurance and Nestle India rounded out the top gainers with rises of 4.58% and 4.03%.

For the sessions ahead, watch whether the Nifty can hold above 25,800 and if market breadth improves beyond the 42-8 split seen in the benchmark. The gap between index strength and broader participation, alongside the Nifty Bank’s 1.79% gain, will indicate whether this rally extends or remains a narrow, defensive-led move.
