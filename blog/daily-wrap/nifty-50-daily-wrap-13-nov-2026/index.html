📝 Market Wrap

Indian equities ended lower on 13 Nov 2026 as financials and cyclical stocks dragged the market down, while a handful of defensive names limited the damage. The Nifty 50 closed at 25,144.3, down 155.05 points or 0.61%. The Sensex slipped 0.39% to 81,590.25, while the Nifty Bank fell 0.79% to 61,034.1 and the Nifty Financial Services index declined 0.72% to 27,855.5. The Nifty Midcap 50 was nearly flat, easing 0.04% to 17,877.45.

The decline was broad rather than confined to a few heavyweights. Within the Nifty 50, 39 stocks fell against just 10 advances. Across the NSE, 1,616 stocks declined compared with 993 advances, while 45 were unchanged. For investors, that breadth reading shows selling pressure extended well beyond the headline index, even though the benchmark’s fall remained below 1%.

Sector performance underlined the cautious tone. Realty led losses with a 1.39% drop, followed by Auto at 1.07%, Commodities at 1.06% and Metal at 0.98%. Bank, Private Bank and Financial Services indices also weakened. Energy edged up 0.03%, while FMCG was almost unchanged at 0.01% lower. Eicher Motors topped the gainers, rising 1.34% to ₹7,650, followed by Hindustan Unilever, up 0.96%. Shriram Finance was the biggest loser, sliding 3.37% to ₹970, while Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles dropped 3.31%.

The next session’s data will show whether weakness remains concentrated in banks, autos and realty or spreads further. Watch the Nifty’s 25,144.3 closing level, market breadth, and whether midcaps continue to resist the broader decline.
