📝 Market Wrap

Banks powered the Nifty 50 to a higher close on 17 Nov 2026, even as more stocks fell than rose across the wider market. The index gained 96.3 points, or 0.38%, to settle at 25,387.4, while the Sensex rose 0.57% to 82,574.4. Nifty Bank climbed 1.13% to 62,273.25 and Nifty Financial Services added 0.64% to 28,195, comfortably outpacing the Nifty Midcap 50’s 0.27% rise to 17,821.3.

The headline gain concealed a divided session. Within the Nifty 50, 27 stocks advanced and 23 declined, a fairly balanced split. Across the NSE, however, 1,172 shares advanced against 1,426 declines, with 56 unchanged. That weaker breadth means the index’s rise was not matched by broad participation, an important distinction for investors whose portfolios extend beyond the largest companies.

PSU Bank led the sectoral table with a 1.95% gain, followed closely by Realty at 1.94%. Bank and Private Bank indices rose 1.13% and 0.89%, respectively, while Auto added 0.72%. At the other end, IT dropped 0.9%, with PSE, Energy, Consumer Durables and FMCG also finishing lower. Adani Enterprises topped the Nifty gainers, rising 3.24% to ₹2,745.1, followed by Eicher Motors, up 3.01% at ₹7,828. Axis Bank gained 1.98% to ₹1,369. HCL Technologies fell 2.09% to ₹1,355.7 and Wipro slipped 1.89% to ₹189.89, underlining technology’s drag.

For the next session, the data to watch are whether banking strength persists, IT stabilises and market breadth improves. A firmer index alongside more declining shares would keep the rally looking selective rather than broad-based.
