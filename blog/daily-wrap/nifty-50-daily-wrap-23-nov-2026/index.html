📝 Market Wrap

A steep selloff in banks and metals dragged the Nifty 50 down 1.75% to 25,349.65, even as technology stocks rallied against the tide. The index lost 450.95 points, while the Sensex fell 2.14% to 82,126.82. Nifty Bank slumped 3.17% to 62,585.35, making financials the centre of the damage rather than a broad collapse across every industry.

The decline was wide. Only 12 Nifty stocks advanced against 38 declines, and across the NSE, 1,886 stocks fell compared with 723 advances, with 48 unchanged. For an ordinary investor, that breadth means weakness extended well beyond the headline index. Yet the Midcap 50 slipped a relatively milder 1.07%, showing that the pressure was uneven.

IT rose 1.69%, with TCS jumping 3.53% to ₹2,460, Tech Mahindra gaining 1.79% and HCL Technologies adding 1.48%. Consumer Durables and Realty also edged up. At the other end, Metal dropped 3.68%, with Tata Steel plunging 8.43% to ₹163. PSU Bank, Private Bank and Financial Services fell between 3.25% and 2.49%; Axis Bank lost 3.97%, HDFC Bank 3.33% and ICICI Bank 3.32%. Shriram Finance’s 2.46% rise stood out within that weak financial backdrop.

The mood was therefore defensive and highly selective, not uniformly risk-off: money favoured a few technology and consumption names while lenders and commodity stocks bore the brunt. Next, watch whether NSE breadth improves, whether banks stabilise near current levels, and if IT’s relative strength persists; those data points will show whether the market’s split personality is narrowing or deepening.
