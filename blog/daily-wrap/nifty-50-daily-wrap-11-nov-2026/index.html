📝 Market Wrap

Banks and autos powered the Nifty 50 to a 210.4-point gain on 11 Nov 2026, even as technology, pharma and realty stocks slipped. The index closed at 25,255.05, up 0.84%, while the Sensex added 0.66% to 81,642.97. Nifty Bank outpaced the benchmark with a 1.76% rise to 60,792.4, and Nifty Financial Services gained 1.02%.

The advance stretched well beyond the headline index. Within the Nifty 50, 36 stocks rose against 14 declines; across the NSE, 1,826 shares advanced, 784 fell and 42 were unchanged. For retail investors, that breadth reading suggests the rally drew support from a wide pool of stocks rather than a handful of heavyweights. Nifty Midcap 50 climbed 0.93%, reinforcing that picture.

PSU Bank led sectors with a 2.94% jump, followed by Auto at 2.14% and Bank at 1.76%. Mahindra & Mahindra surged 5.3% to ₹3,465, while InterGlobe Aviation rose 4.33% to ₹5,665 and Trent gained 3.32% to ₹3,222.7. Consumption, FMCG, energy and financial services also finished higher. The weak spots were IT, down 0.53%, realty, off 0.42%, and pharma, lower by 0.41%. Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone dropped 1.84% to ₹1,907, and ICICI Bank fell 1.1% to ₹1,571.6 despite the broader banking strength. Tech Mahindra and HCL Technologies also declined.

The next sessions’ data will show whether PSU banks and autos retain leadership, ICICI Bank rejoins the financial rally, and IT stops lagging. Watch whether NSE advances continue to comfortably exceed declines and whether midcaps keep pace; those readings will reveal if the positive mood remains broad or narrows again.
