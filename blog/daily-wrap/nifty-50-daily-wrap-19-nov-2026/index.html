📝 Market Wrap

Autos powered a broad rally on Dalal Street, lifting the Nifty 50 by 286.35 points, or 1.14%, to close at 25,514.3, even as technology stocks moved in the opposite direction.

The advance had solid support within the benchmark, with 41 Nifty stocks rising against nine declines. Across the NSE, 1,388 shares advanced, 1,203 declined and 65 were unchanged, indicating a positive but less one-sided session beyond the large caps. The Sensex gained 0.56% to 82,488.26, while the Nifty Midcap 50 rose 0.98% to 17,756.15. Nifty Bank outperformed with a 1.65% rise to 63,063, and Nifty Financial Services added 1.1% to 28,299.65.

Auto was the clear leader, surging 4.4%. Maruti Suzuki India jumped 7.58% to ₹13,589, Eicher Motors climbed 4.66% to ₹8,058.5, Mahindra & Mahindra gained 4.46% to ₹3,509, and Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles rose 4.34% to ₹263.5. Consumer Durables advanced 3.64%, while Hindustan Unilever’s 4.07% rise to ₹2,074.2 complemented gains of 2.09% in FMCG and 2.08% in India Consumption. PSU Bank added 2.29%. IT was the only listed sector in the red, down 1.1%; TCS, Tech Mahindra and Infosys each fell about 1.5%.

For investors, the breadth shows the rally extended beyond a handful of heavyweight stocks, though IT’s weakness tempered the celebration. Next, watch whether advances continue to outnumber declines across the NSE, whether banks and consumption retain leadership, and whether the Nifty holds above 25,500 while IT stabilises.
