📝 Market Wrap

The Nifty 50 surged 2.4% to close at 24,536.6 on 5 Nov 2026, gaining 576 points in a session where financials and automobiles did the heavy lifting.

The rally was broad rather than a narrow large-cap move. Within the Nifty 50, 39 stocks advanced against 11 declines, while across the NSE, 1,762 shares rose, 836 fell and 52 were unchanged. That means gainers comfortably outnumbered losers beyond the headline index. The Sensex climbed 2.63% to 79,338.15, though the Nifty Midcap 50’s 1.65% rise showed that mid-sized stocks participated with less force.

Financial Services led sectors with a 3.99% gain, matching the Nifty Fin Service index’s rise to 26,600.45. Auto jumped 4.14%, Realty added 3.52%, Private Bank gained 3.44% and the Nifty Bank advanced 3.33% to 57,773.65. Bajaj Finance rallied 7.65% to ₹1,070, Shriram Finance rose 6.08% to ₹964, and Mahindra & Mahindra climbed 5.59% to ₹3,127.5. Maruti Suzuki India and Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles also gained more than 5% each.

Defensive and energy-linked pockets lagged: Media slipped 0.84%, Energy fell 0.52% and PSE edged down 0.02%. Apollo Hospitals Enterprise dropped 2.66%, ONGC lost 2.13% and NTPC declined 1.63%. For investors, the mood was firmly risk-on, but leadership remained concentrated in rate-sensitive financial and auto names. Next, watch whether all-NSE breadth stays strong, midcaps catch up and laggard sectors stop dragging.
