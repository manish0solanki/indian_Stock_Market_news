📝 Market Wrap

A powerful rally in technology stocks lifted the Nifty 50 to 25884.5 on 26 Nov 2026, masking weakness in banking and consumer shares. The benchmark gained 65.25 points, or 0.25%, while the Sensex rose 0.47% to 84328.66. The advance was narrow within the blue-chip index: only 22 Nifty stocks rose against 28 decliners. Across the NSE, however, 1336 shares advanced and 1259 declined, with 66 unchanged, indicating that gains extended somewhat beyond the largest companies. The Nifty Midcap 50 outperformed with a 0.61% rise to 17885.1.

IT did the heavy lifting, surging 3.66%, followed by Realty at 3.27% and Metal at 1.17%. HCL Technologies jumped 6.42% to ₹1468.6, Tata Consultancy Services climbed 4.89% to ₹2640.3, and Infosys advanced 4.69% to ₹1183. Wipro and Tech Mahindra gained 3.96% and 2.97%, respectively. That concentration matters: a rising index can flatter a portfolio when a handful of heavyweight stocks supply most of the momentum.

Financials moved the other way. Nifty Bank slipped 0.26% to 63535.3, PSU Bank fell 0.68%, and Bajaj Finance and Shriram Finance dropped 2.96% and 2.84%. Consumer shares also lagged, with Consumer Durables down 1.33%, FMCG off 0.94%, Asian Paints lower by 2.98%, and Nestle India down 2.81%.

The next session’s data will show whether this split persists. Watch if IT retains its lead, banks stabilise, and Nifty advances overtake the 28 declines. A wider gap between NSE advancers and decliners would signal broader participation than the headline gain alone conveys.
