Market Summary — 07 Oct 2026

The mood turned cautious as NIFTY 50 slipped 173 points to close at 22,603, with selling pressure visible across the board—only 9 stocks advanced against 41 decliners. The sharp negative breadth suggests institutional profit-booking rather than stock-specific issues.

Key movers: Kotak Bank jumped ₹8.1 (1.9%) to ₹440 on heavy volumes of nearly 3.8 Cr shares, leading the banking pack alongside ICICI Bank. This signals selective buying in quality private lenders despite the broader weakness. On the flip side, Titan cracked ₹173 (3.8%) to ₹4,377—its worst single-day drop in weeks—likely on valuation concerns after recent run-ups. Hindalco and JSW Steel also bled heavily, dragging the Metal index down 2.3% as global commodity prices softened.

Sector rotation: Money clearly rotated into defensives and rate-sensitive plays. PSU Banks gained 1% while Private Banks held flat. Metals bore the brunt, and even high-flying defence play BEL corrected 2.4%.
