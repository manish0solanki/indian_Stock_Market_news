📝 Market Wrap

A late burst in banks and insurers masked a subdued session for the Nifty 50, which closed at 25,299.35, up 44.3 points or 0.18%. The Sensex moved the other way, slipping 0.47% to 81,496.77, while the Nifty Midcap 50 gained 0.74%. Within the Nifty, 19 stocks advanced and 31 declined, showing that the headline gain rested on a narrow set of heavyweights rather than broad strength.

Financials supplied the lift. Nifty Fin Service rose 1.42%, Nifty Bank added 1.19% and PSU Bank climbed 1.51%. HDFC Life Insurance Company led the gainers with a 4.23% rise to ₹597.8, followed by Shriram Finance, up 3.58%, and Bajaj Finance, up 3.36%. SBI Life Insurance Company gained 2.97% and State Bank of India rose 2.38%. Realty was the strongest sector, surging 4.51%, with Media up 2.46%.

The wider market was healthier than the Nifty’s internals suggest: across the NSE, 1,445 stocks advanced against 1,142 declines, with 66 unchanged. That means more shares rose than fell overall, even as most index constituents lost ground. Weakness clustered in defensives and cyclicals, with Auto down 1.02%, FMCG off 0.74% and Oil & Gas lower by 0.54%. Oil & Natural Gas Corporation dropped 4.39%, Hindustan Unilever fell 2.39% and Nestle India declined 2.1%.

For investors, the split points to selective risk-taking rather than a uniform rally. Next, watch whether Nifty breadth improves, financials and realty retain their lead, and the Sensex stops diverging from the Nifty.
