📝 Market Wrap

Defensive consumer and banking shares carried the Nifty to a higher close even as most stocks across the market fell.

The Nifty 50 gained 146.8 points, or 0.58%, to end at 25291.1. The Sensex rose 0.63% to 82108.4, while the Nifty Bank outperformed with a 0.89% advance to 61575.9. The headline strength was narrow. Within the Nifty, 31 stocks advanced and 19 declined, but across the NSE, 727 stocks rose against 1883 declines, with 44 unchanged. For retail investors, that gap signals weakness beyond the large-cap index. The Nifty Midcap 50’s 0.58% fall to 17774.15 reinforced the divergence.

FMCG led sectors with a 1.87% rise, followed by India Consumption at 1.09%. Bank and Services Sector indices each added 0.89%, while Financial Services gained 0.57%. Tata Consumer Products climbed 3.57% to ₹1117.5, ITC rose 3.54% to ₹295.1, and Nestle India advanced 2.68% to ₹1297. Max Healthcare Institute topped the gainers, jumping 6.63% to ₹1009.9, with Apollo Hospitals Enterprise up 2.87% at ₹8771, despite Pharma and Healthcare falling 1.53% and 0.38% respectively.

At the other end, Metal lost 1.5%, Realty slipped 1.34% and Auto dropped 1.29%. Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles plunged 8.7% to ₹262.4, while Tata Steel, Maruti Suzuki India and Hindalco Industries fell between 2.15% and 2.37%. Next, watch whether NSE breadth improves, midcaps stabilise, and banks and consumption continue offsetting weakness in autos, metals and pharma.
