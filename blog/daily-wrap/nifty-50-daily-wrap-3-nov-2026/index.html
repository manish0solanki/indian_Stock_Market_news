📝 Market Wrap

Indian equities closed firmly higher on 3 Nov 2026, with consumer stocks leading a broad rally. The Nifty 50 surged 370.05 points, or 1.58%, to 23810.85, while the Sensex gained 1.85% to 77092.71. Financial shares also contributed: Nifty Fin Service rose 1.84% to 25357.9, although Nifty Bank’s 0.98% advance to 55540.05 was more restrained. The Nifty Midcap 50 added 1.14% to 16908.3, showing that gains extended beyond the largest companies.

Participation was positive but not uniform. Within the Nifty 50, 38 stocks advanced against 11 declines. Across the NSE, 1440 shares rose, 1160 fell and 43 were unchanged, meaning gainers outnumbered losers without every stock joining the move. FMCG jumped 3.65%, ahead of Realty’s 2.33% and India Consumption’s 2.29%. Private Bank climbed 1.98%, while IT managed only 0.34%. Energy and Metal slipped 0.11% and 0.14%, respectively, and PSU Bank dropped 1.27%.

Nestle India led the gainers, rising 4.47% to ₹1248.4, followed by Tata Consumer Products, up 4.29% to ₹1073, and Asian Paints, up 3.99% to ₹2437.8. Bharti Airtel gained 3.77% to ₹2065 and HDFC Bank 3.64% to ₹725.5. Power Grid fell 3.04% to ₹259.55, State Bank of India lost 1.7% to ₹970.25, and Infosys declined 1.11% to ₹1148.1.

For investors, the session combined strong headline gains with wider market support, but sector leadership remained uneven. Next, watch whether breadth stays positive, PSU banks stabilise, and consumption and financial shares continue to outpace IT, energy and metals.
