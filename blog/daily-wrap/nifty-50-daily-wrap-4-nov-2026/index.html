📝 Market Wrap

Indian stocks extended their rally on 4 Nov 2026, with the Nifty 50 climbing 149.75 points, or 0.63%, to 23,960.6 as gains reached well beyond the headline index. The Sensex rose 0.71% to 77,465.93, while the Nifty Midcap 50 outpaced both with a 1.42% jump to 17,149.1. Banks provided steadier support: Nifty Bank added 0.67% to 55,913.6 and Nifty Financial Services gained 0.88% to 25,580.6.

The advance was broad rather than dependent on a handful of heavyweights. Within the Nifty, 35 stocks rose against 15 declines; across the NSE, 1,980 shares advanced, 613 fell and 55 were unchanged. For retail investors, that breadth indicates participation across the market, not merely an index flattered by a few large constituents.

Media led sectors with a 5.15% surge, followed by Realty at 2.85%, Metal at 2.8% and Energy at 2.17%. Consumer Durables, PSE, Commodities, Oil & Gas and FMCG also posted solid gains. Bharat Electronics topped Nifty gainers, rising 4.71% to ₹398, while Adani Enterprises advanced 4.47% and Titan 4.22%. Jio Financial Services and Max Healthcare gained 3.73% and 3.53%, respectively.

Technology stood apart. IT fell 1.5%, with TCS down 2.52%, HCL Technologies 2.37%, Wipro 1.74% and Infosys 1.73%; Coal India also slipped 2.05%. The split suggests a risk-on mood favouring domestic cyclicals and midcaps while software exporters lagged. Next, watch whether breadth remains strong, midcaps keep outperforming, and IT stops dragging as the Nifty holds near 23,960.6.
