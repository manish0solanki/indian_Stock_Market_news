Market Summary — 04 Sept 2026

Markets traded mixed with Nifty 50 ending flat at 23,897.7, up just 24 points. More stocks fell than rose — 27 declined against 23 gainers — signalling underlying caution despite the headline number.

Insurance stocks stole the show. SBILIFE surged 3.5% to ₹1,775 and HDFCLIFE jumped 2.4% to ₹546.4, both riding strong volume. This matters because life insurers are seeing renewed investor interest after months of underperformance — a potential trend reversal worth tracking. TATASTEEL (+2.49%) led the metal pack, helping that sector top the charts with +1.07%.

On the flip side, IT bellwether HCLTECH dropped 1.94% and BHARTIARTL shed 1.55%, dragging sentiment. MARUTI fell 1.27% on auto concerns. Realty was the worst hit, down 0.9%.

Sector rotation is evident — money is moving from defensives like IT and telecom into beaten-down financials and cyclicals like metals. Banking barely held ground, down 0.02%.
