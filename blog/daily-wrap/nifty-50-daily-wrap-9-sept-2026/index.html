Market Summary — 09 Sept 2026

The mood turned cautious as NIFTY 50 shed 203 points to close at 23,431.5, dragged down by weak global cues and heavy selling in IT stocks. Market breadth was poor with just 13 advances against 36 declines.

IT was the day's worst hit, crashing 3.24% across the board. Infosys led the rout, plunging ₹47 (-4.34%) to ₹1,035 — its sharpest single-day fall in months — as worries over US spending cuts and visa rule tightening spooked investors. HCL Tech and Tech Mahindra also bled over 3% each. This is a wake-up call: the sector that carried markets through 2024-25 is now facing headwinds.

On the flip side, metals shone with a 1.79% rally. Tata Steel gained 2.5% to ₹188.75, while Coal India rose 2.56% to ₹431, riding China stimulus hopes and firm domestic demand. The Adani pack flexed again — Adani Enterprises surged 5.13% to ₹3,104.7 and Adani Ports added 3.8% to ₹1,775, bucking the weak trend on strong operational updates.

Clear rotation underway: money is moving out of expensive tech and into beaten-down cyclicals and commodities.
