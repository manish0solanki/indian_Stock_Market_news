Price Action & Technicals

PRICE ACTION: TCS posted its sharpest single-day advance in recent sessions, with volume surging to more than triple the daily average, indicating genuine institutional participation rather than thin-market noise. The stock remains deep in bearish long-term territory, trading 35.6% below its 52-week peak, though today's bounce halts the short-term slide.

What's Driving the Stock?

  • Driver 1: The decisive close above the 20-day SMA at ₹2119 breaks a sequence of lower highs and suggests short-term selling pressure has exhausted, at least temporarily.
  • Driver 2: The 3.47x volume spike on an up-day reflects accumulation interest; such conviction often precedes a sustained relief rally rather than a one-off spike.
  • Driver 3: The 3.85% gain outpaced the broader IT index, indicating relative strength that could attract momentum-focused participants if sustained.

Key Risks

  • Risk 1: The 50-day SMA at ₹2254 sits just 4.5% higher and has acted as dynamic resistance; failure to clear it would confirm the bounce as a dead-cat rally within a broader downtrend.
  • Risk 2: With the stock still 35.6% off its 52-week high, overhead supply from trapped long-term holders could cap rallies near ₹2200-2250.

The Bottom Line

The setup has shifted from weak to mixed, with short-term momentum turning constructive but the intermediate trend still bearish. The critical test is a sustained move above the 50-day SMA; until then, treat the bounce as tentative and watch for volume drying up on follow-through sessions.
