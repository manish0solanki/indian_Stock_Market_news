Market Summary — 28 Aug 2026

Markets closed bullish with NIFTY 50 reclaiming 24,175, up 84 points. Breadth favoured advances — 30 stocks rose against 19 decliners.

IT stocks stole the show, surging 3.51% and single-handedly pulling the index higher. TCS led from the front with a ₹93.6 jump to ₹2,342, while Infosys added ₹33.2 to close at ₹1,144 with strong turnover of ₹1,096 Cr. The entire top-5 gainers list was packed with IT names — Tech Mahindra, HCL Tech and Wipro all rallied 2.5-3.5%. This suggests fresh buying in software exporters, possibly on improving US deal flow optimism.

On the flip side, FMCG was the lone weak spot, slipping 0.46%. ITC shed ₹3 to ₹266, and Asian Paint lost ₹22.3. Financials saw selective profit-booking — ICICI Bank dropped ₹20.2 despite heavy ₹1,231 Cr turnover, and Shriram Finance fell 1.28%. Metals and healthcare posted modest gains, but the clear rotation was money moving out of defensives and financials into beaten-down IT counters.
