Market Summary — 24 Aug 2026

Markets stayed cautious as NIFTY 50 closed flat at 24,219, down just 33 points. The split breadth — 23 stocks up, 26 down — shows investors are treading carefully.

Metals shone brightest. JSW Steel jumped ₹33 (2.6%) to ₹1,327, while Hindalco gained ₹24.5 (2.4%). Both hit strong volumes, suggesting real buying interest, not just speculation. This metal rally likely reflects hopes of China stimulus lifting global commodity demand — a theme worth tracking.

Financials faced selling pressure. Bajaj twins — Bajaj Finance and Bajaj Finserv — fell over 1.5% each. SBI Life also slipped 1.7%. This weakness in NBFCs and insurance hints at profit-booking after recent runs, not structural worry.

Sector rotation is visible. Money moved from financials and PSU banks (-0.9%) into beaten-down metals and realty (+0.6%). IT held steady with HCL Tech up 1.5%, showing defensive appeal.
