Market Summary — 25 Aug 2026

Markets closed on a firm note with the NIFTY 50 reclaiming the 24,300 mark, up 115 points at 24,334. Breadth stayed healthy with 34 stocks advancing against 16 decliners — a clear bullish tilt.

Healthcare stole the show, surging 1.09% and contributing three of the day's top five gainers. Apollo Hospitals jumped ₹187 to ₹8,889 while Max Healthcare added ₹25.5 to cross the ₹1,000 milestone. This pharma-hospital rally signals renewed defensive buying after recent consolidation. On the other side, private banks dragged marginally (-0.2%) with HDFC Life slipping 1.13% — a mild profit-booking blip rather than structural worry.

The Adani pack roared back. Adani Enterprises vaulted 3.72% to ₹3,110 on strong volumes of over 20 lakh shares, while Adani Ports gained 1.83%. Indigo joined the party, up 2% to ₹5,218, riding aviation optimism.

Sector rotation is evident — money is flowing from rate-sensitive private banks into healthcare and consumption plays. PSU banks (+0.75%) and IT (+0.57%) offered steady support.
