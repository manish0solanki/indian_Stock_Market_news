Market Summary — 03 Sept 2026

Markets stayed cautious today with Nifty 50 slipping 41 points to close at 23,873. Advance-decline ratio favoured bears at 21:29, showing weak breadth despite a narrow index decline.

HDFCBank rallied ₹5.85 to ₹706.65 on heavy volumes of nearly 2.8 crore shares — worth ₹1,967 Cr in turnover. This heavyweight single-handedly cushioned Nifty's fall and signals institutional comfort in private banking. AdaniPorts surged 2% to ₹1,706.5, riding the realty/infrastructure wave with strong delivery volumes.

Sector rotation was the day's story. Realty led with a sharp 2.58% jump, followed by Media at 1.74%. Private and PSU banks held firm. Meanwhile, IT dragged with TechM down 1.54%, as global tech spending worries linger. Bajaj-Auto crashed ₹210 to ₹11,920 on weak export data chatter.

The split market — financials green, IT and autos red — suggests investors are cherry-picking defensives and rate-sensitive plays over global-facing sectors.
