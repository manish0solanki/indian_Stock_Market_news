🎯 What Happened

The Nifty Auto index staged a powerful 4.14% rally on 5 Nov 2026, adding 1096.95 points to close at 27601.75, with heavyweight stocks setting the pace.

The advance was broad but uneven. Of the 122 NSE-listed companies in this industry group, 87 rose, 34 fell and one was unchanged, giving a breadth score of 43.4. The top ten companies by market capitalisation gained 3.9% on average, well ahead of the 1.52% average for smaller stocks. The market-cap-weighted rise of 3.64% also outstripped the median gain of 1.54%, showing that larger companies did more to lift the index than the typical stock.

UNO Minda led the gainers with an 11.54% jump, followed by Ashok Leyland at 10.5%. Menon Bearings rose 9.05%, Rolex Rings 8.92% and JK Tyre & Industries 8.74%. The rally did not reach every corner of the sector: NRB Bearing dropped 8.57%, S.J.S. Enterprises fell 5.93%, Uniparts India 5.6%, Sintercom India 4.2% and Ather Energy 3.34%. For an ordinary investor, the gap between the index surge and the median move matters: a strong headline gain can overstate the experience of a portfolio holding smaller or weaker auto stocks.

Next, the data to watch is whether advances remain spread across the 122-stock universe and whether the smaller-stock average closes the gap with the large caps. The distance between the index close and its previous close of 26504.8, alongside the persistence of sharp individual losers, will show whether the rally broadens or remains concentrated.
