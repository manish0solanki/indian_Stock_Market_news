🎯 What Happened

The Nifty Realty index surged 4.51% to 934.25 on 12 Nov 2026, but the rally’s real story was the sharp divide between heavyweight developers and the rest of the pack. The index gained 40.3 points from its previous close of 893.95, while the ten largest stocks by market capitalisation averaged a 4.92% rise. Smaller stocks managed only a 1.11% average gain, and the median stock rose just 0.84%, showing that the typical realty share lagged the headline move by a wide margin.

Breadth was positive without being emphatic. Across the wider universe of 78 NSE-listed companies in this industry group, 49 advanced, 27 declined and two were unchanged, producing a breadth score of 28.2. For an ordinary investor, that means gainers comfortably outnumbered losers, but the strong index reading did not translate into an equally strong session for every property stock. The market-cap-weighted change of 4.01% also trailed the large-stock average, underlining how much the leaders shaped the day.

Prozone Realty led the gainers with a 19.98% jump, followed by Aditya Birla Real Estate at 10.16%. Raymond Realty advanced 7.33%, Marathon Nextgen Realty 7.22% and Max Estates 6.51%. At the other end, Ravinder Heights slipped 3.47%, Thakkers Developers 3.07%, HB Estate Developers and Pansari Developers 2.81% each, and Peninsula Land 2.04%.

The next data points to watch are whether the index holds above 934.25, whether advances broaden beyond 49 stocks, and whether the smaller-stock average closes the gap with the 4.92% large-cap gain. A narrowing gap between the median move and the index would signal a more evenly shared rally.
