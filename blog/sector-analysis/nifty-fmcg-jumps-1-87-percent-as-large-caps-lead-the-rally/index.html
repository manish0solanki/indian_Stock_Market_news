🎯 What Happened

The Nifty FMCG index jumped 1.87% on 16 Nov 2026, but the headline masked a weak session for most stocks in the wider industry group. The index closed at 47708.1, up 874.55 points from 46833.55, even as 57 of the 96 NSE-listed companies in the universe declined. Only 38 advanced and one was unchanged, leaving a breadth score of -19.8. For an ordinary investor, that negative reading means the rally was not broad-based: more shares lost ground than gained, despite the strong index move.

Large companies did the heavy lifting. The top 10 stocks by market capitalisation rose 1.77% on average, while smaller stocks fell 1.05% on average. The market-cap-weighted gain of 1.16% also contrasted with the median stock’s 0.56% decline, showing that bigger names had a much better day than the typical company in the group.

Among the standout gainers, Parag Milk Foods rose 3.9%, followed by Krishival Foods at 3.86% and Mrs. Bectors Food Specialities at 3.83%. Two large consumer names also featured prominently: Tata Consumer Products gained 3.57% and ITC advanced 3.54%. At the other end, Associated Alcohols & Breweries dropped 8.61%, the sharpest fall in the list. KN Agri Resources slipped 6.25%, Godavari Biorefineries lost 6.09%, India Glycols declined 6.06% and Kriti Nutrients fell 5.51%.

The next data points to watch are whether breadth improves beyond 38 advancers, whether smaller stocks close the gap with the 1.77% large-cap average, and whether gains remain concentrated in a handful of heavyweight names.
