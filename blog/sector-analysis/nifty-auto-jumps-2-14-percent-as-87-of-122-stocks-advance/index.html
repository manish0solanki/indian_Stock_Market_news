🎯 What Happened

Auto stocks powered ahead, with the Nifty Auto index jumping 2.14% to 28,523.3 and gains stretching well beyond the biggest names.

The advance added 597.4 points from the previous close of 27,925.9. Across the wider universe of 122 NSE-listed companies in this industry group, 87 advanced, 34 declined and one was unchanged. That breadth score of 43.4 means winners comfortably outnumbered losers, rather than a handful of heavyweights flattering the headline. The median stock rose 1.04%, so a typical company participated, though less strongly than the index.

Large caps set the pace by a narrow margin. The top 10 by market capitalisation averaged a 2.11% gain, against 1.09% for smaller stocks; the market-cap-weighted change was 1.72%. For an ordinary investor, that combination points to a rally led by bigger companies but supported across the pack, not a fringe-only surge.

The sharpest moves still came from individual names. Jinkushal Industries rallied 9.64%, followed by Talbros Automotive Components at 7.51%, Ramkrishna Forgings at 7.19%, PPAP Automotive at 6.91% and Modi Rubber at 6.84%. The other side was concentrated in smaller auto-ancillary counters: Sintercom India slipped 6.18%, MM Forgings 4.55%, Menon Bearings 4.48%, Lakshmi Precision Screws 3.79% and Sundram Fasteners 2.91%.

Next, watch whether breadth stays this favourable, whether the gap between large and smaller stocks narrows, and whether the index holds above 28,523.3. The losers’ list also matters: persistent weakness there would show the rally remains uneven beneath a strong headline.
