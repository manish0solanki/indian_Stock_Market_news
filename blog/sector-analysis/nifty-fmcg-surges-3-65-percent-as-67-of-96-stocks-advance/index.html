🎯 What Happened

The Nifty FMCG index surged 3.65% on 3 Nov 2026, closing at 45,580, as heavyweight stocks powered one of the sector’s strongest sessions in the data.

The rally was broad but uneven. Of the 96 NSE-listed companies in this industry group, 67 advanced, 27 declined and two were unchanged, producing a breadth score of 41.7. That means gainers comfortably outnumbered losers, but the average move does not tell the full story. The median stock rose just 1.14%, well below the index’s gain, while the market-cap-weighted change was 3.29%.

Large companies clearly led. The ten biggest stocks by market capitalisation averaged a 3.98% rise, compared with only 0.85% for smaller stocks. Marico stood out with a 10% jump, followed by Tasty Bite Eatables at 8.12% and Britannia Industries at 6.36%. Piccadily Agro Industries gained 6.1% and Ganges Securities 5.19%. The index added 1,605.35 points from its previous close of 43,974.65, showing how strongly the larger movers pulled the sector higher.

There were still sharp pockets of weakness. Raj Oil Mills fell 8.44%, Sanwaria Consumer lost 5%, Comfort Intech slipped 4.9%, JHS Svendgaard Laboratories declined 3.5% and Krishival Foods dropped 3.19%. For investors, the contrast between strong headline gains and a modest median rise shows that the rally did not lift every stock equally. The next data points to watch are whether breadth remains supportive, whether smaller stocks narrow the gap with large caps, and whether the index holds above its previous close.
