🎯 What Happened

Heavyweights dragged the Nifty Auto index down 1.48% on Thursday, masking a session where most auto stocks actually gained ground.

The index closed at 28,199.9, shedding 423.55 points from the previous close of 28,623.45. The split was stark: the ten largest stocks by market capitalisation averaged a 1.77% decline, while smaller stocks averaged a 0.64% gain. The market-cap weighted change of -1.16% shows how the biggest names pulled the benchmark lower even as the broader pack held up.

Breadth tells the real story. Of 122 NSE-listed companies in this industry group, 76 advanced against 44 declining, with two unchanged. The median stock rose 0.54%, meaning a typical auto share finished higher despite the index slump. The breadth score of 26.2 captures that underlying strength. For retail investors, this gap matters: an index fall driven by a few large names feels very different from one where most holdings are bleeding.

LG Balakrishnan & Bros led gainers with a 6.05% jump, followed by MM Forgings at 4.91% and Escorts Kubota at 4.77%. Omax Autos and Mercury Ev-Tech added 4.75% and 4.17% respectively. On the other side, Hyundai Motor India slid 4.67%, Endurance Technologies dropped 4.06%, and Bajaj Auto fell 3.36% — declines in index heavyweights that explain the divergence.

Watch whether the large-cap weakness persists or narrows toward the broader pack, and whether advancing stocks continue to outnumber decliners. A sustained gap between the median stock and the weighted index would signal the headline number is understating sector health.
