🎯 What Happened

A brutal 3.68% plunge in the Nifty Metal index masked a sharp split, with heavyweights tumbling while a clutch of smaller steel-tube makers rallied hard.

The index closed at 11,819.25, down 451.85 points from the previous close of 12,271.1. Selling was broad across the 126-stock universe: 99 declined against just 27 advancers, with none unchanged. That left a breadth score of -57.1, a reading that tells retail investors the fall was not confined to one or two names. Yet the damage was uneven. The top 10 stocks by market capitalisation fell 1.84% on average, smaller stocks slipped just 0.83%, and the market-cap-weighted change was -2.27%. The median stock dropped 1.29%, far less than the headline index.

APL Apollo Tubes led the losers with a 21.29% crash, while Tata Steel sank 8.43%, dragging the large-cap end. Deccan Gold Mines lost 6.42%, SG Mart fell 5.49% and Hariom Pipe Industries declined 4.25%. At the other end, Welspun Specialty Solutions surged 15.2%, Hi-Tech Pipes jumped 13.51% and Sambhv Steel Tubes rallied 11.92%. Ratnaveer Precision Engineering gained 8.93% and Euro Panel Products added 4.74%, showing that pockets of the metals space shrugged off the rout.

For ordinary investors, the gap between the index fall and the milder median decline signals that big stocks carried most of the weight. Next, watch whether breadth improves from 27 advancers, if large-cap losses extend beyond Tata Steel and APL Apollo Tubes, and whether the smaller gainers hold their moves.
