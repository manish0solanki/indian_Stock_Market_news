🎯 What Happened

The Nifty Consumer Durables index suffered a sharp 3.13% decline on 18 Nov 2026, closing at 37288.8, as heavy losses in large-cap stocks dragged the sector lower despite pockets of resilience in smaller names. The index shed 1205.1 points from its previous close of 38493.9, with the market-cap weighted change matching the overall drop at -3.13%. This underperformance was driven by the top 10 stocks by market capitalisation, which averaged a 3% decline, significantly worse than the 0.72% average fall seen in smaller stocks.

Market breadth painted a bleak picture for ordinary investors, with declining stocks vastly outnumbering advancers across the 106-stock universe. Only 29 stocks advanced while 76 declined and one remained unchanged, resulting in a breadth score of -44.3. This negative reading signals that the selling pressure was broad-based rather than confined to a few heavyweights, meaning most portfolios exposed to this sector likely felt the pinch. The median change of -1% further confirms that the typical stock in the group ended in the red.

Among individual movers, Greenlam Industries stood out as the top gainer, rallying 10.61%, followed by KDDL which rose 6.94% and Wonder Electricals which added 4.43%. Ethos and Advit Jewels also bucked the trend with gains of 4.11% and 3.9% respectively. On the losing side, Relaxo Footwears slumped 6.48%, Shanti Gold International dropped 6.16%, and both Archidply Industries and Paramount Communications fell 5.81%. Titan Company, a major large-cap name, declined 5.3%, contributing significantly to the index's weakness.

Investors tracking this space will want to watch whether the negative breadth persists in coming sessions and if the divergence between large-cap weakness and smaller-stock resilience continues. The ability of the index to hold above current levels while breadth remains poor will be a key data point to monitor.
