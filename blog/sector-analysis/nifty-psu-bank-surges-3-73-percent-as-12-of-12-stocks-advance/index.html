🎯 What Happened

The Nifty PSU Bank index staged a powerful rally, closing at 8905.35 with a gain of 3.73%, or 320.25 points, over the previous close of 8585.1. This was not a move driven by one or two heavyweights. All 12 stocks in the NSE-listed universe advanced, with none declining or unchanged, giving a breadth score of 100. For an ordinary investor, that reading signals a clean sweep: gains were shared across the group rather than concentrated in a narrow pocket.

Large lenders led the charge. The top 10 stocks by market capitalisation rose 3.42% on average, well ahead of the 1.13% average gain in smaller stocks. The market-cap weighted change of 3.28% was below the index’s 3.73% rise, while the median stock gained 4.02%, showing that the typical lender had a strong session even as the biggest names set the pace. Union Bank of India topped the gainers with a 5.6% jump, followed by Punjab National Bank at 5.27%. Bank of India added 4.53%, Bank of Baroda 4.46% and Indian Bank 4.35%.

The laggards still finished higher, underlining the breadth of the move. Indian Overseas Bank rose just 0.71%, while UCO Bank and Punjab & Sind Bank gained 0.92% each. Central Bank of India advanced 1.33% and Bank of Maharashtra 1.74%.

Next, watch whether the index holds above 8905.35 and whether breadth remains close to 100. The gap between large-cap and smaller-stock gains is another key marker: a narrowing gap would show the rally spreading further, while a widening one would keep the spotlight on the leaders.
