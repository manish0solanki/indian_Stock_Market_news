🎯 What Happened

Nifty Media suffered a sharp sell-off on Tuesday, plunging 4.44% to close at 1,671.5 as heavyweight stocks dragged the sector lower despite resilience in smaller counters. The index shed 77.7 points from its previous close of 1,749.2, marking a decisive break in momentum.

The decline was overwhelmingly concentrated in large caps, with the top 10 stocks by market capitalisation averaging a 3.7% fall. In stark contrast, smaller stocks in the universe averaged a marginal 0.02% gain, highlighting a clear divergence between market leaders and the broader pack. The market-cap weighted change of -3.59% underscores how heavily the biggest names influenced the index’s trajectory.

Breadth data revealed the extent of weakness, with 36 stocks declining against just 16 advances and three unchanged, producing a breadth score of -36.4. This negative reading indicates that selling pressure was widespread across the 55-stock universe, even as select counters bucked the trend. The median change of -0.61% suggests the typical stock fell far less than the headline index, pointing to outsized losses in a few heavyweights skewing the overall picture.

Sun TV Network led the losers with a 9.21% drop, followed by Nazara Technologies at 7.93% and Sambhaav Media at 7.02%. Zee Entertainment Enterprises and Saregama India also featured among the top decliners, falling 6.18% and 5.63% respectively. On the gaining side, Vertoz surged 16.4%, while Balaji Telefilms rose 5.19% and Hindustan Media Ventures added 4.61%.

Investors should watch whether breadth improves in coming sessions and if large-cap weakness persists, as these factors will determine whether the index stabilises or extends losses.
