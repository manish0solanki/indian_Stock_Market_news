🎯 What Happened

Private banks surged on 6 Nov 2026, with the Nifty Private Bank index jumping 3.22%, or 920.6 points, to close at 29503.4 from 28582.8, powered decisively by the biggest lenders.

The rally was solid but not universal. Of the 20 NSE-listed companies in this industry group, 13 advanced and seven declined, with none unchanged, giving a breadth score of 30. For an ordinary investor, that means gainers clearly outnumbered losers, but the advance was not an across-the-board sweep. The median stock rose 1.59%, well below the index’s gain, showing that the headline move was stronger than the typical stock’s performance.

Large banks did the heavy lifting. The top 10 by market capitalisation averaged a 2.17% rise, while smaller stocks averaged just 0.41%. The market-cap-weighted change of 3.46% confirms that the biggest companies pulled the index higher. Yes Bank led the gainers with a 5.91% jump, followed by ICICI Bank at 4.75%, HDFC Bank at 3.72%, Axis Bank at 3.35% and Kotak Mahindra Bank at 2.78%. With ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank and Axis Bank all rising sharply, heavyweight strength was central to the day’s story.

The laggards were concentrated among smaller lenders. Dhanlaxmi Bank slipped 1.8%, CSB Bank fell 1.65%, The Karnataka Bank declined 1.47%, The Jammu & Kashmir Bank eased 0.82% and IDFC First Bank lost 0.6%. Next, watch whether breadth improves beyond 13 advancers, whether smaller stocks close the gap with large caps, and whether the index holds above its previous close of 28582.8.
