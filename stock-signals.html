How signals are calculated

Trend filter

EMA 20 / EMA 50 alignment and higher-timeframe direction.

Momentum confirmation

RSI and MACD must support the direction; indicators alone never trigger a trade.

Volatility-aware risk

ATR and recent swing structure define the invalidation level. Targets are calculated from actual risk.

Quality gates

Freshness, OHLC consistency, minimum reward-to-risk and liquid-symbol eligibility must pass.

Decision-support software, not a guarantee of returns. Signals can lose money. Validate historical performance and paper-trade before risking capital. Prices may be delayed.
