Import mutual funds from CAS

A Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) lists every mutual fund linked to your PAN, across all fund houses, with every purchase, SIP and redemption. It's free and arrives by email as a password-protected PDF.

  1. Open the CAMS statement page below. It covers funds serviced by both CAMS and KFintech.
  2. Choose Detailed (includes transaction listing) and period Specific period from the earliest date to today.
  3. Enter your PAN and the email registered with your mutual funds, and set a password. You'll need it to upload here.
  4. Submit. The PDF usually reaches your inbox within minutes.
Request on CAMS ↗ KFintech ↗ MF Central ↗
MarketPulse can't request the statement for you: CAMS sends it only to the email registered with your funds, which keeps your holdings private.
The statement is read on this device. The PDF and its password are never uploaded or stored; only the fund transactions you confirm are saved to your portfolio.

Import Portfolio

Supports both transaction files and holdings snapshots. For holdings reports, MarketPulse reads Script Name, Quantity, Avg Unit Cost, and Status, resolves the NSE symbol, and creates an initial BUY position. Current Price / Current Value / Unrealized P&L are recalculated from the live price feed. Because a holdings snapshot has no original trade date, imported rows use today's date and are marked as CSV snapshot imports. Existing transactions are not deleted.
