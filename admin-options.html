How trades are picked and closed
  • Source: the Stock Signals scanner's current BUY / SELL signals, F&O stocks only. Signals in a weak sector (Sectors page filter) are skipped.
  • Trade: BUY → bull call spread (buy the at-the-money call, sell the call at the strike nearest the signal's target 1). SELL → bear put spread, the mirror image. One lot, one spread per stock at a time.
  • Expiry: the nearest with at least 14 days left, so time decay doesn't dominate.
  • Liquidity: both legs need a live bid and ask, a bid-ask spread within 12% (bought leg) / 20% (sold leg) of the price, and open interest of at least 50 contracts.
  • Price: skipped when the bought option's implied volatility is above 1.6× the stock's 20-day historical volatility (options unusually expensive), or when the maximum profit is less than 0.8× the maximum loss.
  • Fills: conservative: buy at the ask and sell at the bid on entry, the reverse on exit. Brokerage and taxes are not included.
  • Exit: the stock hits the signal's stop, the stock reaches target 1, the spread is worth 80% of its width, or 2 days are left to expiry. The most a spread can lose is the debit paid.
