Questions

Who publishes the signals and picks?

You do. The platform generates and tracks rule-based setups and lets you publish under your registration; you review and approve what goes out. It is software for your research business, not advice from us.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Your site runs on your domain with your logo and colours; MarketPulse is not shown unless you choose to.

Are my subscribers' accounts separate from other analysts'?

Yes. Each client has a separate user base: an account made on your site works only on your site, and you can see your own user list.

What about market data?

Showing exchange prices on a public website needs a market-data licence. For your site we arrange one through an authorised data vendor and bill it at cost, or you can use your own licence.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

Monthly plans can be cancelled any month. Annual plans get two months free. Founding-partner pricing is locked for 12 months.
