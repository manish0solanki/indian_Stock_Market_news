Events calendar

Results, dividends, bonus issues, splits and buybacks on NSE. Click any event for the details.

Events come from NSE's event calendar and corporate actions, refreshed hourly. For tracked stocks (the scanner's 219 stocks) and stocks in portfolios, results are read from the company's NSE filing once it is out, compared with the previous quarter and the same quarter last year, and checked against how the shares moved. The verdict is a rule-based summary of those facts, not a forecast, and trade signals come from the Stock Signals scanner. The Economy dates (RBI policy, India CPI and GDP, the Union Budget, US Fed decisions and US CPI) come from the official calendars; dates marked “Expected date” follow the usual schedule and are not yet confirmed. Times are IST. Nothing here is investment advice.
